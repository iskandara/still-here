### LOW: decoration and noise

6. **Banner comments:** 15 boxed `/* ======…====== */` headers (14 app, 1 landing), many in ALL CAPS (`STATE`, `DERIVATION`, `UI  RENDER`, `BOOT`). Fix: one plain sentence-case line. Where a banner holds a real fact (the Appwrite config note, the homestead API signature), keep the fact as plain lines. *(R-31)*
7. **Dashed section labels:** 37 `/* ---------- tracker ---------- */`-style CSS and JS headers and 34 `// ---------- ground ----------`-style canvas headers. 9. **Shouting words inside comments:** "MIGRATE the current forest", "NEVER the habit, the target…", "NOTE: the sky gradient…" (twice). The rules are worth keeping; the capitals aren't. Fix: sentence case. *(R-31)*
10. **Emoji in a comment:** `// ---------- Ciki 🤍 — a beloved companion who joins once the farm is alive ----------`. Fix: `// Ciki, the dog who joins once the farm is alive`. *(R-04)*
11. **History inside a comment, written by me last round:** `/* dark chip keeps the growth message readable over the pale sky (was 1.07:1) */`. The "(was 1.07:1)" is history, not a fact about the code. Fix: drop the parenthesis. *(R-31)*
