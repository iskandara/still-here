# antislop audit 003: simpler, more elegant, more exclusive, still playful (2026-11-01)

Mode: AFTER (session override). Skills read: `antislop`, `-ui`, `-copywriting`, `-human`, `-layoutmobile`, plus the `awwwards` notes as a source of ideas only (antislop wins on conflicts, per CLAUDE.md).

Scope: `index.html`, `about.html`, `app/index.html`. Nothing has been changed. How I checked: read all three files, ran the pages in a browser at 1440 px and 390 px, scrolled the whole landing page, filled in the app's first-run form and logged a first stick, and ran the contrast checker on the main text pairs.

## Design read

Reading this as: a free habit-reduction app and its story, for people who tried and failed at cold turkey, in a cozy plum, honey and leaf pixel-island style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 (from CLAUDE.md).

What the dials look like in practice today:

- ENERGY 2: holds. The island, the plum base and the honey accent carry it.
- RHYTHM 2: does not hold. Five sections use the same "heading left, card or list right" split and four use equal-weight panels, so the real rhythm is 1.
- MOTION 2: does not hold on the landing page. A pinned scroll scene that needs 3.6 screens of scrolling before the headline appears is MOTION 3 (R-19).

## What is already good (keep it)

- The island is the identity. Swap the logo and it is still unmistakably this product (R-20 passes). Everything below is about giving it more room, not replacing it.
- Honesty is real: made-up charts carry an "Example" tag, no fake stats, no testimonials, no fake logos, no generic FAQ. The one "Coming soon" is labelled.
- Contrast passes where I measured it: muted text on panels 6.4 to 7.5:1, button text on honey 5.9:1, on cendol green 5.0:1.
- Keyboard and motion basics: visible honey focus ring, Escape closes the menu, `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured, no horizontal overflow at 390 px, tap targets are 44 px.
- Sentence-case labels, no uppercase tracking, no bento, no mesh gradients, no new libraries. The earlier audits did their job.
- The app's home screen is already the best-edited part of the product.

The problem is not slop. It is volume: too many sections saying the same thing, too many equal-weight cards, and a few cute habits (emoji, chunky gradients, shadows, pixel type at small sizes) that make it read friendly rather than exclusive.

## Findings

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW. Items marked ★ move your goal (simpler, more exclusive) the most, whatever their tier.

### HIGH

1. **Illustrative numbers sit beside the chart without their own "Example" tag.** In the Insight section the list says "53% of your logs", "about 8 a day", "28% lower" as plain statements. Only the panel on the right is tagged "Example". Someone reading the left column first meets numbers that look like a claim. Same pattern on the sanctuary tiles ("Arrived" / "Locked") and the ladder (six rungs shown as reached). Fix: tag the list the same way (or fold the sentences into the example panel), and say "Example" once per group. *(R-17, R-38)*

No other Hard Gate failures found. Em dashes: none in visible text; the 2 left in `app/index.html` are developer `console.warn` strings, as noted in fix report 002.

### MEDIUM

2. ★ **Emoji used as interface icons.** Landing: 🌏 🇮🇩 on the donate buttons, ☰ and ✕ as menu glyphs. Story page: 📍 on the location line, 🌏 🇮🇩 again. App: 🔥 🏡 💨 📸 ✦ ⚙ on the home screen, plus many more in sheets. Emoji render differently on every device, clash with the pixel art, and are the cheapest signal on a page. Fix: draw the few marks that matter in the same pixel style as `assets/sprites/` (flame, house, coin, camera, gear), or use text only. Those would be new assets, so I'd ask you before making any (R-23). *(R-04)*
3. ★ **The hero hides the headline and the button.** With JS on, the first screen is just the empty island and "Scroll to grow your island". The headline, the lead and "Plant your first day" only fade in after about 2.5 screens of scrolling (`.js-intro .intro{height:360svh}`, copy opacity driven by `--copy`). The idea is the best thing on the site, but it asks for patience before saying what the product is, and a first-time visitor on a phone may never get there. Fix: keep the growth-on-scroll island, cut the pinned length to roughly 200 to 220 svh, and show the name and one short line plus the button from the first frame, so the scroll is a bonus rather than a gate. *(R-19, MOTION dial)*
4. ★ **Chunky gradient buttons everywhere, two of them competing in the footer.** Every button is a honey gradient with a 3 to 5 px hard offset shadow, and the footer adds a green gradient beside a gold one. The offset shadow is a real identity gesture (it reads like a game key). The gradient on top of it is extra. Fix: solid honey, keep the single hard offset as the one playful gesture, make the second donate button a quiet outline, and use one primary action per screen. *(R-01, R-12, R-13)*
5. **Soft shadows on cards and the floating header.** About 10 `box-shadow` declarations on the landing page, half of them large drop shadows: nav icon, nav capsule, intro icon, homestead card, story photo, and more. The page already has a strong ground plane (the dark plum). Fix: remove them from everything except the hard-offset button, and let one thin line (`--line`) do the separating. The island sprite already supplies all the depth the page needs. *(R-12)*
6. **Near-duplicate green.** `--leaf:#8fce6b` and `--cendol:#8ed36a` are the same colour to the eye, plus a second green gradient for the footer button. With plum, honey, leaf, coral and the sky canvas that is already five hues. Fix: merge to one `--leaf`; keep coral only as data colour in the charts. *(R-29)*
7. ★ **Pixel type is used at small sizes.** Pixelify Sans is great at 34 to 52 px. It is also used for h3s at 15 to 19 px, ladder labels, coin rows and card captions, where its stepped edges get busy and the "fi" ligature trouble you already patched shows up. Fix: Pixelify for the H1/H2 and for numbers only; Nunito 800 for everything smaller. That is the single biggest "elegant" lever and needs no new asset. *(R-06)*
8. **Text at 10 to 12 px.** 10 declarations on the landing page, 62 in the app (chart labels, captions, the "Example" tag, ladder notes). Contrast passes, but at these sizes it breaks the 200% zoom and low-vision expectation. Fix: 13 px floor for anything a reader has to read; 12 only for pure chart ticks. *(R-25 spirit, antislop-human zoom)*

### LOW

9. ★ **Eleven sections that say the same few things.** Landing is about 8,650 px on desktop and about 11,000 px on a phone. "Slips never destroy it" is said six times (problem, honest-not-punishing, homestead, rewards, how it works, final). The Insight charts repeat the "Your months" and calendar panels. The sanctuary panel repeats the forest section. "How it works" repeats everything above it. Proposed order, each section earning its place: hero, one short promise (problem plus the tone toggle), the forest (stages, with sanctuary creatures folded in), two more things grow (homestead and coins side by side, one line each), insight (real charts, once), your story teaser, final line and footer. About 6 sections instead of 11. *(R-05, C-3)*
10. ★ **Same composition repeated.** Insight, homestead, rewards and how-it-works are all a two-column split with a heading left and a card or list right; the four dashboard panels are equal cards with the same gradient, radius and padding. This is Uniform section rhythm and Copy-paste feature cards. Vary on purpose: let the homestead scene run full width, put the coins into a single sentence, keep one panel treatment only for the actual app previews. *(R-05, R-14)*
11. **The rewards section sells a store price list to a first-time visitor.** "Saturn sky, 2500 coins", mangoes, a kite: this is app depth, not a reason to try. Fix: one sentence ("Honest days earn coins. Spend them on skies and trees. Coins are never taken away.") and at most two swatches, or move it into the app only. *(C-3)*
12. **Seven small labels above headings.** "Kind by design", "Insight", "Watch it come alive", "A second thing grows too", "A third thing grows too", "How it works", "Why I made this". Each restates what the H2 already does, and "second thing / third thing" is a template chain. Fix: remove all of them; where one carries a fact, put it in the headline. *(R-31, R-09 spirit)*
13. ★ **Five nav links for a one-page site.** Insight, The forest, Rewards, How it works, About, plus the button. With sections cut (finding 9), three links are enough (The forest, Insight, About) plus Open app. On a phone the floating home icon has no backing, so text slides under it (visible around the sanctuary pill at 390 px); give it the same solid chip as the capsule or drop it on small screens. *(R-03, C-3)*
14. **Copy that sounds like a template or a pitch.** Examples: "Everything you need to see progress." (generic), "Three small things." (forced list; the second one is the same as the first), "No zero. No shame. Just tomorrow." (staccato run), "forgives the ones you stumble on" (a forest that forgives), a missing comma in the problem paragraph ("slip which, for a habit"), "give support by clicking the link below :)", and on the story page "Hi hehe" and a double space in "thought  of". Fix: shorter, plainer, quieter lines, in your own voice, which is the real source of "exclusive" here. I will propose exact wording for you to approve rather than invent it. *(R-16, copywriting skill)*
15. ★ **The app home screen holds nine controls.** Top: home chip, coin pill, phase pill. Camera button on the island. Bottom: run chip, two progress cards, the main button, four icon buttons. Inside the sheet: four tabs, then Challenges/Store sub-tabs. Fix: home = island, one line ("3 of 7 today"), one button, and a small handle that opens everything else; merge Today and Month into one line and move Sanctuary/Challenges/Insight/Oath into the sheet's tabs. *(C-3, R-20)*
16. **The install-to-home-screen dialog opens right after the first oath**, before the first log, on top of the island you just planted. It breaks the best moment of the product. Fix: wait until after the first good day or the third visit. *(R-19, C-1)*
17. **First-run form has four stacked fields plus a three-way toggle.** Fix: one sentence-style oath ("I want to cut [thing] from [now] to [goal] per [day / week / month]") with sensible placeholders. Same data, less form, more voice. *(C-3)*
18. **Outside badge and external hosts.** The "Featured on 21st Tools" image is loaded from a third-party host and is the least on-brand thing on the page. I can't verify the listing, so please confirm it is real. If it is, a plain text link in the footer does the job without the loud badge. *(R-36, R-20)*
19. **App links all open in a new tab** (7 `target="_blank"` on the landing page). For a PWA this strands people with no back path. Fix: same tab. *(C-2)*
20. **`background-attachment:fixed` on the body.** Known to stutter on iOS and flagged by the awwwards notes as dated. The status-bar fix you already added covers the colour; the gradient can scroll with the page. *(R-19)*

## Left as is on purpose

- The story page layout (one column, photo, quote, oath card). It is the quietest and most "exclusive" page already. The honey left bar on its quotes marks quoted text, so it carries meaning (R-01 pass).
- Sprites and the island art, the dusk scene, the builders and cabin.
- The tone toggle ("Gentle / Plain / Honest"). It is real, tiny and charming: keep, but move it into the promise section.
- The "Example" tags on charts. The honesty is a feature.

## Out of scope here

- The about page's personal wording (the "smoking hot" aside). That is your voice and your story, so I haven't touched it.
- I did not run the app past the first log in a real cloud session (login and sync are Appwrite and the sandbox blocks outside hosts). Findings 15 to 17 are from the first-run and home screens only.
