# antislop audit 001 (2026-10-07)

Mode: AFTER (session override). Direction: the current Still Here identity, confirmed by the owner (plum + honey + leaf palette, Pixelify Sans pixel type, cozy isometric pixel island).

Reading this as: a personal habit-reduction landing page and web app for people cutting down rather than quitting, in a cozy pixel-art island style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Scope: `index.html` (landing), `about.html` (story), `app/index.html` (app onboarding and home screen). Checked at 1280px desktop and 390px phone, with contrast measured by `contrast-check.py` against pixels sampled from screenshots.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **Hero caption is unreadable.** "This little island is yours to grow. Give it a good day." and every "a new life arrives" message under it are honey `#f4b860` on the pale sky, measured at **1.07:1** (needs 4.5:1). This is the main feedback when someone taps "Plant a good day". *(R-25)*
2. **"Still Here / YOUR QUIET FOREST" watermark on the sky is unreadable,** about 1.3:1, in the landing hero card and in the app's top-left header. On the landing page it also repeats the brand already in the nav. *(R-25)*
3. **Mobile menu can't be used by keyboard properly.** Escape doesn't close it (tested), the close button "✕" has no label for screen readers, the ☰ button doesn't report open/closed, and focus stays behind the overlay. *(R-32)*
4. **No designed focus indicator.** None of the pages has a `:focus-visible` style. In the app, the inputs remove the outline (`outline:none`) and only change a 1px border colour. *(R-32)*
5. **Tap targets under 44px on phone:** ☰ 40×40, "Open app" 92×37, "☀ Day" 61×28, donate buttons 41 tall; in the app, "Sanctuary" and "Oath & tone" are 41 tall. *(R-03)*
6. **Em dashes in UI copy:** the page title, the "Gentle" tone quote, the calendar caption, the journey intro, the "Haven, at dusk" caption, the demo's final message, and many app strings (for example the install popup's "like a real app — full-screen"). *(R-02)*
7. **Em dashes in your own story (`about.html`)** ("a pack a day — twenty cigarettes, give or take —", "— made with love", meta description). This is your writing, so per R-37 I'm not changing it silently: **keep them or replace them?** *(R-02, R-37)*
8. **The sample dashboard looks like real data and the caption describes something that isn't there.** The months chart (190/180/110/80/62) and calendar have no "example" label, and the caption says "The green line is your goal", but no line is drawn. *(R-17, R-38)*
9. **The app has no `<!doctype html>` and no `<meta charset="utf-8">`.** It runs in quirks mode, and when served without a UTF-8 header every emoji and dash turns into "â€”" / "ðŸ”¥" (this happened in my local test). The viewport also blocks pinch zoom (`user-scalable=no, maximum-scale=1`), which locks out people who need to zoom. *(C-4, R-35)*

### MEDIUM

10. **Journey cards float up and down forever** (`animation: float 6s infinite`), and nothing on the site respects "reduce motion". The hero island moving is the product; the cards bobbing is decoration. *(R-19)*
11. **The sanctuary panel uses emoji (🦋🐓🐇🦊🦄🐉) when you have real pixel sprites** for the same animals in `assets/sprites/`. The sprites are your identity motif; the emoji look like every other site. The "🌱" on the hero button has the same issue (`tree_sprout.png` exists). The 🌏 / 🇮🇩 on the donate buttons stay, because they tell people which one is international and which is Indonesian. *(R-04)*
12. **Blur and glow on small pills.** `backdrop-filter` on the nav, mobile menu, "Sapling" pill and Day button (4 places; the two pills are 90% opaque so the blur does nothing), plus a glowing dot in the phase pill. Keep the nav blur and drop the rest. *(R-10, R-13)*
13. **Pixel font breaks "fi".** Pixelify Sans renders "first" as "Arst" in the about page's pull quote ("For the first time I can remember…"). Turning off ligatures on Pixelify text fixes it everywhere. *(R-06, readability)*

### LOW

14. **"How it works / Three small things" is the template 3-step block** (round number badges 1-2-3 in identical cards), and it comes after the forest and homestead sections have already shown the same ideas. Keep the words, drop the numbered circles and cards, and show them as a plain three-line list. *(R-05)*
15. **Uppercase wide-tracked eyebrow above every section** ("KIND BY DESIGN", "WATCH IT COME ALIVE", "A SECOND THING GROWS TOO", "HOW IT WORKS", "WHY I MADE THIS"), mostly repeating the headline. Switch to sentence case without the wide tracking. *(R-06)*
16. **Decorative arrows** on "Open the app →" (mobile menu) and "Open the app to keep growing →". Keep the arrow only on "Read the full story →", which goes to another page. *(R-08)*
17. **Leftover dead code:** an empty `.trust` div in the hero and unused `.herobadge` / `.kind` / `.kcard` CSS. *(C-1)*

## Checked and passing (reasons written down, R-31)

- Palette: plum base, honey accent, leaf for "good"; it's your brand and the accent sits on the CTAs. *(R-01, R-29)*
- Typography: Pixelify Sans for headings matches the pixel-art forest; Nunito for body is rounded and friendly, matching the gentle tone. *(R-06)*
- Dark theme: a cozy dusk forest is the brand mood, not "tech". *(R-21)*
- Chunky 3D button shadow and the two big card shadows (hero island, homestead) mark the interactive scenes. *(R-12)*
- Honey left bar on the about pull quote marks a quotation, which is a real function. *(R-31)*
- No fake testimonials, statistics, logos, or FAQ. Nav links all go to real sections. *(R-18, R-24, R-28)*
- No horizontal overflow at 390px on any page. *(R-03)*
- Body text contrast is good (muted on plum 7.5–8:1, button text 7.7–8.3:1). *(R-25)*
