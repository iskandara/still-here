Your forest, your data.

Privacy at Still Here · Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version

  • There are no ads, and I never sell or share your data.
  • Your forest is stored with Appwrite in Singapore so it can sync. Only your sign-in can open it.
  • The analytics don't use cookies. They count clicks and visits, never what you track or how much.
  • You can delete your forest any time from the app's settings. To remove your account too, write to aradnaksi@stillhere.day.

Who runs this

Still Here is made by one person: me, Aradnaksi, in my spare time. There's no company behind it. If you have a question about your data, write to me at aradnaksi@stillhere.day and I'll answer it myself.

What the app stores

The first time you open the app, it creates a guest sign-in for this browser. You don't give a name or an email for that. Your forest is saved under that guest sign-in, and it holds:

  • your oath: what you're cutting down, what you count, your starting rate and your target
  • the date and time of every tap you log
  • which days you stayed within your limit, plus your coins, store items and settings
  • any name you give a companion in your forest

If you make an account so your forest follows you to other devices, the app also stores your email, your password, and the name and favourite animal you enter. Appwrite handles the password. I never see it.

If you send feedback, I get your message and, only if you add it, your email so I can reply.

Where it lives, and who can see it

Everything above is stored with Appwrite, a database service, in its Singapore region. Your whole forest is one record, and the app sets it so only your sign-in can read, change or delete it. Other people using Still Here can't see it.

As the person who runs the project, I can open the database from Appwrite's dashboard. I don't browse people's forests. I'd only open yours if you ask me to, for example to delete it or to fix something that went wrong.

If the app can't reach Appwrite, it carries on in this browser only: your forest is saved in this browser's storage and sent nowhere. The sync line in the app's settings shows which one you're on.

Analytics

I use Umami to see whether people find the site and use the app. It doesn't use cookies. For each visit it records the page, the site you came from, your browser, your device type and your country.

It also counts a few actions, so I can tell which parts are useful: opening the app from the home page, scrolling to a section of the home page, logging a tap, making an account, sharing a picture, getting a store item (which item), tapping a support link, and asking for a password reset.

Analytics never include your oath, your counts, your name or your email. A logged tap shows up only as "a tap happened", with nothing about what you track.

Other services your browser talks to

Like any website, these services see your IP address and basic browser details when your browser loads something from them:

  • Vercel hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces.
  • jsDelivr serves the code the app uses to talk to Appwrite.
  • Appwrite sends the email when you ask for a password reset.

If you buy me a coffee or a cendol, that happens on Ko-fi or Trakteer under their own privacy policies. I don't get your card details.

In your browser

Appwrite keeps a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in. When the app runs without Appwrite, it also keeps your forest in your browser's storage, and "Delete my forest" clears that copy too. There are no advertising cookies.

When you share a picture of your forest, the app draws it on your device and nothing is uploaded. Your goal is left off the picture unless you turn on "Show my goal when sharing" in the settings.

Keeping and deleting

I keep your forest and account until you ask me to delete them. Nothing is deleted automatically.

"Start the forest over" in the app clears your logged days and your homestead. It keeps your account, your name, your coins and your oath if you choose to.

When you log out, the app starts a fresh guest forest in that browser. Your account's forest stays safe until you log back in.

"Delete my forest" in the app's settings erases your forest from Appwrite straight away: your oath, every logged tap, your coins, store items and names. It works for guest forests too. Afterwards the app starts you on a blank forest, and if you had an account, it logs you out.

The app can't remove the account itself (your email and the name you signed up with). To remove that as well, email aradnaksi@stillhere.day from the address on your account, and I'll delete it and reply when it's done.

You can also ask me for a copy of your forest, or ask me to delete feedback you sent.

If this page changes

If the app starts storing something new or using another service, I'll update this page and the date at the top before it happens.
